Weigh in the morning after the bathroom, before eating. 3 times a week is enough. Look at the trend, not single days.

Daily targets

8–10kSteps per day. This does most of the fat loss work.
~2,200kcal per day, a moderate deficit (estimate).

7+ hSleep. Recovery decides how fast you progress.

Calories are a starting estimate. Weigh yourself 3 mornings a week and average it. Aim to lose 0.4–0.7 kg per week. If the average stalls for 2 weeks, cut about 150 kcal or add 2k steps.

How to progress

  • Leave 1–2 reps in reserve on every set. Stop before form breaks down.
  • When you hit the top of the rep range on all sets, add the smallest weight jump next session.
  • Missed a day? Do not double up. Just continue with the next workout.
  • Short on time? Drop the last isolation exercise (curls, calves, abs) and keep the big lifts.
  • Week 1 should feel easy. Learn the movements first.
  • Rest 2 minutes after heavy compound lifts and 60–90 seconds after isolation work.
